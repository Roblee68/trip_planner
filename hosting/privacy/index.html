PB&B

Privacy policy

Effective date: November 12, 2026
Operator: Myomesh Technologies Corp
Privacy contact: hello@myomesh.ca

PB&B helps you discover activities and events. This policy explains what the PB&B app and its supporting service handle during the beta.

Information you choose to provide

You can set a city and country, interests, travel options, currency, distance and budget. You can save or pass ideas, save event details and screenshots, and ask for reminders. These preferences and saved items are kept on your device. They are not synchronized across devices in this beta.

When you request a live search, PB&B sends the details needed for that request to its service: your chosen place, relevant preferences, search words, and up to 12 titles of ideas you marked as loved. If you ask PB&B to read a screenshot or public link, the selected image or link is sent for that request. We use these inputs to return suggestions and do not intentionally store the submitted image in the PB&B database.

Location

If you tap Use my current location, the app asks for foreground location permission. After approval, it refreshes your position while you actively use the app, resolves your nearby city and country, and sends approximate coordinates (rounded to roughly 100 metres) to PB&B’s service for distance checks. For route research, approved approximate origin coordinates and the confirmed destination are also sent to OpenAI. Precise GPS fixes stay in app memory and are not saved in the preferences file or published in the shared event catalogue. The app does not track you in the background or keep a location history. You can decline permission or enter another place manually. Your device’s configured timezone is read separately to interpret time-sensitive requests; event records retain each event’s own timezone.

When you choose Look up destination, the text you enter is sent through PB&B’s service to Photon, a geocoding service operated by komoot using OpenStreetMap data. This can include an address you enter. Results are shown for you to confirm before route research starts. Destination lookup results and confirmed locations are cached privately with your app identifier for up to seven days; expired records are periodically removed, and account deletion removes these records. Private route-research responses are cached with your identifier for thirty minutes and removed after their retention period. Location verification does not use the AI allowance. See the Photon service and OpenStreetMap attribution and licence.

Guest identity, optional accounts, alerts, and service records

Firebase creates an anonymous guest identifier so the app can call the service and enforce search limits. You can optionally create an account with an email address and password or use Sign in with Apple on iPhone. Firebase handles account credentials and may receive your email address or an Apple relay address. PB&B does not store your password. If you turn a guest identity into an account, its identifier is retained. The service keeps usage counts tied to that identifier. It also keeps shared area event caches, which are refreshed periodically. Saved ideas stay on the phone and are not synchronized across devices.

If you turn on daily scout alerts, PB&B stores your chosen place, currency, budget, travel radius and modes, interests, time zone, and push token with your guest identifier. It uses those details to look for possible matches and sends alert text and your push token through Expo's push service. It keeps delivery records so it can avoid duplicate alerts and check delivery status. Turning daily alerts off removes the active alert subscription. You can decline notification permission.

PB&B Social preview and planned service

PB&B Social is currently a private, on-device preview. Its nickname, self-reported gender choice, selected travel cities, sample requests and group messages stay in this phone’s app storage; they are not sent to other members or the Social service. The Canadian city suggestions are bundled with the app, so typing a city in this picker does not send that search to a location provider. Signing out or deleting an account clears the phone’s Social preview data. The preview removes event groups more than thirty days after their event when it next opens.

If live Social is launched, it will require a verified account, an active Social membership and a separate Social opt-in. The planned service would store a nickname, self-reported gender choice, the cities a member selects for travel, notification preference and push token if enabled, event companion requests, group membership and messages, and safety reports. It would not copy the member’s PB&B home-place entry or GPS coordinates into a Social profile. Accepted group members would see group messages; PB&B operators may review reports and related content to address safety issues. Group chats would be stored by PB&B, not end-to-end encrypted.

Planned Social alerts would use generic lock-screen text. If enabled, the push token and generic alert would pass through Expo’s push service. Turning Social off or deleting an account would remove that person’s Social profile, requests and messages. Event groups and chats would be removed thirty days after the event. Safety-report retention and the final membership and moderation process will be documented before live Social is enabled. A further privacy update and choice to join will be provided before the live service starts.

Our cloud services may also receive technical data needed to operate the app, such as an IP address, device or app information, and error logs. We use that data for delivery, security, troubleshooting, and abuse prevention.

Providers and external sites

PB&B uses Google Firebase for guest and optional account authentication, cloud functions, data storage, and search limits; Apple for Sign in with Apple when chosen; OpenAI to process live searches and selected screenshots or links; and Expo to deliver optional push notifications. These providers may process information outside your country. The PB&B cloud functions are deployed in Toronto, Canada; that does not mean every provider processes data only in Canada.

Each event card links to an organizer or other source. Opening a source takes you to that site's own service and privacy practices. PB&B does not control those sites or guarantee that an event remains available.

Organizer event submissions

On the organizer website, a representative may create an account with a business email and send us a legal and public business name, contact name, business phone and address, optional registration number, website, event details and official event link, and up to three image links. Firebase handles the account password and email verification. We record the submission time, account identifier, email, review status, image-permission choice and version, and any optional sponsorship or future-email preference. These records are private to PB&B reviewers; a submitted event is not published automatically. We use them to check business identity, event facts, image rights, and whether to include the event or respond to a requested sponsorship inquiry. We do not give organizer contact details to app users or other advertisers. We do not currently accept image-file uploads on this form; reviewers inspect the linked images before use.

An organizer can ask us to correct or remove a submission or withdraw image permission by emailing hello@myomesh.ca from the verified business address. We keep submissions while needed for review, the event listing, safety and fraud checks, and related records, then remove them when no longer needed, subject to any legal requirement. Selecting “maybe later” alone does not sign up the business for promotional emails; that requires a separate optional choice.

Advertising

The beta has no third-party advertising tracker and does not sell individual user profiles. Example cards marked sponsored are illustrative; no business has paid for those placements. If this changes, we will update this policy and provide any choices required before using data for a new purpose.

Retention and deletion

Saved items and screenshots remain on your phone until you remove them or uninstall the app. Shared area event caches are refreshed on a weekly schedule, though old cache records may remain in storage until deleted. The beta currently has no automatic deletion period for usage counts and notification delivery records. We retain those service records while needed for the beta's limits, alert delivery, security, and troubleshooting, and remove them when no longer needed or when a valid deletion request is completed. An active daily-alert subscription remains until you turn it off or it is removed because its push token no longer works.

If you created an account, open Settings → Account → Delete account and data to delete the account, its service usage records and alert subscription, and its saved items on this device. If you use PB&B as a guest, or need help with a data request, email hello@myomesh.ca. We may need an identifier from your app to find the correct guest record and verify that the request is yours. Uninstalling the app deletes its local data but does not by itself delete server-side records. Some service records may need to be retained for security or legal reasons; we will explain any exception in our reply.

Security, children, and changes

The app sends requests to its service over HTTPS. We limit access to the systems needed to operate the beta. No online system can guarantee absolute security.

PB&B's external beta is intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information, contact us at hello@myomesh.ca.

We will post an updated policy when our practices change and give notice or choices where required.
