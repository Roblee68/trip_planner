Common questions

How do I change my area or interests?

Open Settings in the app to choose your place, interests, travel options, distance, and budget.

Why do I need to check an event’s source?

Activities, prices, dates, and availability can change. Open the organizer’s linked page and confirm the details before making plans.

How do reminders work?

Reminders for saved finds require notification permission on your device. Check PB&B’s notification settings and your device settings if a reminder does not appear.

How do I ask about my data?

Read our privacy policy or email us. If you are asking about server-side data, include the Privacy request ID shown near the bottom of Settings so we can find the right guest record.

How do I report an image or listing?

Email hello@myomesh.ca with the image or listing, its source link, and why it should be changed or removed. We will review the report and remove or replace material when appropriate. A source link or credit does not mean the organizer has endorsed PB&B.
